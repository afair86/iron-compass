One responsibility. Seven days of practice.

A practical pack for the week you actually have.

Use paper or type into this worksheet. Your answers stay in this tab and are not sent anywhere. They are not saved automatically: print or use your browser’s Save as PDF before closing.

1. Choose one responsibility

Choose something that already needs you this week. Make it specific enough to act on: prepare tomorrow, finish one overdue task or give someone your full attention.

2. Name the action

Choose one action within your control. Give it a time and a clear finish. Keep the commitment realistic for the week you actually have.

3. Plan for a difficult day

Write a smaller version you can still carry out when time or energy is short. If you miss it, record what happened and choose the next available opportunity. A miss is information, not a cancelled week.

4. Keep a seven-day record

Each day, note whether you kept the full commitment, kept the smaller version or missed it. Add one factual line about what helped or got in the way. You do not need a perfect streak.

5. Review the week

Look at what happened before adding more commitments. Keep what worked, change what did not fit, and make one clear commitment for the week ahead.
