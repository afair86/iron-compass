IRON COMPASS AI — DAILY DISCIPLINE CHECKLIST

Use this on paper or in your own notes. No account is needed.

Date:
What is mine to take care of today?
What does completion look like?
When can I realistically act?
What will I change if the day is disrupted?
What actually happened, and what needs following up?

Five-minute preparation (suggested allocation):
1 minute: name the responsibility.
2 minutes: write one action with a clear finish.
1 minute: choose a realistic opportunity.
1 minute: decide how to respond if that opportunity disappears.

Preparation is separate from doing the task. Do not cut necessary rest or assume
another person can cover an unagreed duty to make the plan fit.
