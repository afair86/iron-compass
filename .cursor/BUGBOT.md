# Bugbot — Iron Compass website

This is the public site, not the app database. Do not review Prisma, credit charging, or challenge tables here.

Priority: false claims, broken paths into the app, search regressions, phone layout, privacy, legal pages, prices, and the existing design.

## Always check

- A page describes One Move, Trajectory, Pressure Radar, Ask My Life, the ledger, or challenges as available when the app product state says they are not live.
- "Journal" is used in a way that confuses the public articles with the private app journal.
- A link to `/app` or a domain page is dead.
- The title, description, canonical URL, sitemap, or robots file was broken.
- The phone layout hides the heading or the next step.
- Privacy or terms changed the operator, contact email, or ABN without that being the task.
- A price does not match Free $0, Core $12 a month, or Pro $29 a month.
- Analytics or a form collects more than the privacy page allows.
- A route was removed or duplicated.
- Images or scripts became much heavier without a reason.
- Text is hard to reach by keyboard or a screen reader.
- Colours, logos, or type drift off the dark, blue, and restrained-red system.

## Do not treat as a defect

- Writing style when the claim is true and the page works.
- An article that teaches a behaviour without saying the app already does that job.
