# Website decisions

Do not reverse these because a headline would be easier.

## 2026-09-27 — Do not market a plan as if it is live

**Decision:** A feature is described as available only when the live app has it. Planned work stays off the page.

**Reason:** The site and the app were drifting into two products.

**Revisit if:** The app product state changes, and the live URL shows the feature.

## 2026-09-27 — One brand

**Decision:** The website uses the same philosophy, reliability muscles, voice, and domain names as the app. It does not invent a harsher or softer brand.

**Reason:** Men should recognise one Iron Compass.

**Revisit if:** The founder writes a new voice guide for both repos together.

## 2026-09-27 — Prices match the app

**Decision:** Any public price must match the app: Free $0, Core $12 a month, Pro $29 a month. No price page until the founder asks.

**Reason:** The website roadmap still marks a public price page as pending. The app already charges those amounts.

**Revisit if:** The founder changes the app prices first, then this site.

## 2026-09-27 — Legal pages name the real operator

**Decision:** Privacy and terms must name the operating entity that actually runs Iron Compass. Do not leave two different operators in public.

**Reason:** The live privacy page and the app privacy page do not currently say the same thing. That disagreement is reported, not silently edited.

**Revisit if:** The founder confirms one operator, one email, and one ABN, and asks for both sites to be updated.

## 2026-09-27 — The site points at the app

**Decision:** This site explains and invites. The app holds the private journal, promises, and challenges. Do not rebuild those here.

**Reason:** Two copies of the product would diverge.

**Revisit if:** The founder asks for a specific public tool that is not the private app.
