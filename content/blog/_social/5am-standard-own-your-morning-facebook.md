## Facebook primary post (copy/paste)

Most morning routines die the first week your roster moves.

The 5 A.M. Standard is not a purity test about waking at five forever.
It is one rule: own the first protected block of your day before the world spends you.

Wake cue. Body online. One standard. One next move.
If nights or overtime hit, shorten the block — do not abandon the identity.

I wrote the full protocol for shift work, overtime, and overloaded weeks here:
https://ironcompassai.com/blog/5am-standard-own-your-morning

Save it for your next roster reset.

## How to post in Meta Business Suite (founder steps)

1. Open https://business.facebook.com → **Meta Business Suite**
2. Go to **Content** → **Create post** (or Planner → Create)
3. Select your **Facebook Page**
4. Paste the **primary post** text
5. Upload the **square image** from the path above (or let the link pull the hero)
6. Add the link if not already in the caption
7. Post now — or schedule for 6:30–7:30am local on a weekday
8. After it posts, add the **pin comment**

Optional: cross-post to Instagram from the same composer if connected.
